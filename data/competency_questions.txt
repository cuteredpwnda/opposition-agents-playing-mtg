# MTG Ontology — Competency Questions
# These questions guide KnowledgeGraphBuilder's iterative discovery loop.
# Each question represents something the knowledge graph should be able to answer.

# === Card Lookups ===
What is the mana cost and oracle text of a given card?
What keywords does a given card have?
What creature types does a given creature have?
In which formats is a given card legal?
What is the EDHREC rank and salt score of a given card?

# === Combos ===
What combos include a given card?
What cards are needed to assemble a given combo?
What effect does a given combo produce?
What are the setup requirements for a given combo?
How fragile is a given combo (how easily disrupted)?
What combos produce infinite mana?
What combos result in an instant win?

# === Synergies ===
What cards synergize with a given card?
What is the synergy strength between two given cards?
What tribal synergies exist for a given creature type?
What mechanic-based synergies exist for a given keyword?

# === Archetypes & Strategy ===
What archetype does a given set of cards suggest?
What are the signature cards of a given archetype?
What win conditions does a given archetype typically use?
What strategy (aggro, control, combo, etc.) does a given archetype employ?
What are the weaknesses of a given archetype?
What archetypes are strong against a given archetype?

# === Counter-Play & Answers ===
What cards effectively counter a given threat?
What is the best timing to deploy a given answer?
How effective is a given card as an answer to a given threat?
What removal options exist for indestructible creatures?
What answers exist for a given combo?

# === Card Interactions & Rulings ===
How do two given cards interact with each other?
What are the official rulings for a given card?
What comprehensive rule sections apply to a given interaction?
Does a given card trigger another given card's ability?

# === Deck Building ===
What cards are in a given decklist?
Who is the commander of a given Commander decklist?
What archetype does a given decklist belong to?
What format is a given decklist built for?

# === Metagame ===
What is the current metagame share of a given archetype?
What are the top-tier archetypes in a given format?
How has the metagame shifted after recent bans?

# === Opponent Modeling ===
Given an opponent is playing a control archetype, what counterspells might they have?
Given cards already seen, what is the likely archetype of the opponent?
What cards are typically in the hand of a player running a given archetype?
What should I play around based on the opponent's open mana and archetype?
